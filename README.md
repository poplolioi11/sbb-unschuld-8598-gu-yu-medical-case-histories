# 〔醫案〕（古愚，柏林國立圖書館 Unschuld 8598） / Yi an [Medical Case Histories]: Transcription Dataset

《〔醫案〕》是柏林國立圖書館藏 Unschuld 8598 的十九世紀中國手稿，館方著錄作者為古愚。卷首保存法香、請神和符式，後續大部分為個別醫案，記錄病情、脈象、方藥、改方與服藥後續。有數段內容近似、用字或旁注有異的診語，可供比對實際的複診與手稿抄錄痕跡。

This nineteenth-century Chinese manuscript of medical case histories is attributed to Gu Yu in the Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin catalogue, shelfmark Unschuld 8598. Opening ritual and talismanic material precedes case notes with symptoms, pulse descriptions, prescriptions and follow-up observations. Similar case notes on separate leaves preserve differences in wording, formulae and marginal additions.

來源共109幅，收正文、小字、刪改及符式定位。兩幅僅見反向透印，末幅為附色卡的首葉重拍，逐幅保留定位。

The 109-image witness includes case records, small writing, corrections, and talisman locations. Two frames show bleed-through only and the final frame is a color-target repeat of the first leaf.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

逐幅回圖，比對獨立讀稿及原初錄，採用 48 項回圖訂正。疑讀、藏印或細小批注按實際可辨範圍保留。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。相似病例依不同葉分開，保留側記與手寫劑量符號的疑讀。字、符式與紙損區分標記。

Source-image review compared independent observations with the initial transcription and records 48 image-supported changes. Uncertain readings remain marked. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Similar records on separate leaves retain their distinct readings and annotations. Doubtful characters, handwritten quantity signs and non-textual marks remain flagged.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
